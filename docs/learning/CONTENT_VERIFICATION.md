# Content Verification — ChemLab Learning

## Verification levels

| Level | Meaning | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `internal` | Written by the ChemLab team (AI-assisted). Equations machine-checked for element and charge balance; numeric answers recomputed by `verify()`; answers graded by the engine in tests; requirements paraphrased from the 2018 programme; facts cross-checked against the sources listed in the lesson. **Not reviewed by a teacher or subject expert.** | All 6.4 lessons and questions |
| `ai-draft` | Written but not yet through the checks above. | Not published |
| `teacher-reviewed` | Reviewed and signed off by a chemistry teacher. | None yet |
| Legacy | Short content from 6.2 guided activities; not re-reviewed. | Lessons not yet rewritten |

"Verified" is never shown to learners for `internal` content; the lesson metadata panel states that no independent review has happened.

## What the automated checks cannot prove

These need a teacher's review before content is labelled `teacher-reviewed`:

- Whether explanations match the depth and wording expected by the KNTT textbook and the 2018 programme's requirement statements (paraphrased from secondary copies, the official text was not opened).
- Qualitative chemistry claims (colours, observations, uses, safety notes) — checked against general references only.
- Whether difficulty labels (Nhận biết → Vận dụng cao) match how teachers would classify the item.
- Data choices: textbook-rounded atomic masses (Cl = 35,5, Cu = 64…), gas volume 24,79 L/mol at 25 °C and 1 bar, and standard potentials rounded to 0,01 V (Fe²⁺/Fe kept at −0,44 V; CRC gives −0,447 V).
